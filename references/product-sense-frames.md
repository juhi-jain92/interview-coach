# Product Sense Frames (P1-P6)

The answer pattern for every product sense question: state the framework, ask
clarifying questions, make a CONDITIONAL recommendation. Never jump to a
solution. The coach grades whether diagnostic thinking came before answers.

## P1: Fall in love with the problem, not the solution

Trigger: "Build a product using [trendy tech]" / "Design a feature for X."
Move: resist features, reframe to user and friction point first, then evaluate
whether the tech is the most elegant solve. Ask: target user and workflow,
specific pain, current workarounds, what success looks like.
Red flag: "I would build X" before naming the user and need.
Metrics: high CAC + low retention = problem users don't feel; low adoption =
wrong problem.

## P2: The value metric

Trigger: "Revenue flat, rethink monetization" / "grow ARPU."
Move: find the exact unit where the customer experiences ROI (completed ride,
2,000 messages, successful checkout) and align pricing to that moment.
Watch: contraction MRR = pricing misaligned; NRR > 110% = aligned; high
engagement + low ARPU = under-monetized.
Red flag: "add a premium tier" without asking what behavior pricing should
incentivize.

## P3: Escaping the feature factory

Trigger: "Big customer wants X or they'll churn."
Three diagnostics every time: (1) Is this even our domain? (2) Are others
asking? (3) Why are THEY asking; what outcome do they want?
Then: separate requested solution from underlying problem; isolated ->
workaround; widespread and north-star-moving -> scalable platform capability.
Outcomes over outputs: behavior change, not features shipped.
Watch: 30-day adoption, usage breadth/frequency, roadmap outcome hit rate.

## P4: The PMF mirage

Trigger: "300% acquisition growth, flat revenue."
Move: audit retention cohorts before spending more; leaky bucket check.
PMF decays via market evolution, competitive compression, cohort expansion.
Watch: retention curve must flatten; Sean Ellis under 40% = at risk; cohort
revenue expansion direction.

## P5: Growing revenue

Trigger: "How do you 2x the business?"
Step 1: Revenue = Users x ARPU. Step 2: baseline audit (retention stable?
market penetration? under-monetized?). Step 3: conditional paths:
- Volume play (strong retention, open market): onboarding friction, loops,
  referrals. Acquisition tiers: high-intent abandoners (fix the leak, best
  ROI) -> adjacent personas (expand use case) -> upmarket (deep monetization).
- Value play (mature market, retained power users, low ARPU): premium tiers,
  upmarket, value-metric realignment.

Prioritization pattern (the property-manager example): importance x current
satisfaction. Commoditized needs with satisfying deterministic tools
(payments, accounting) lose to high-importance, low-satisfaction messes
(maintenance tracking). Lead the MVP with the underserved gap.

## P6: Time-to-value

Trigger: "70% drop within 48 hours."
Move: frame as TTV, not marketing. Nail the activation definition (the action
correlating with retention), audit every step between signup and that moment,
defer or remove anything that doesn't advance it. Target value in under 60
seconds (Zoom, Canva, ChatGPT patterns).
Watch: activation rate, drop-off by screen, median time-to-first-value.
Red flag: treating early churn as acquisition or quality before auditing
whether users ever reached core value.
