# Scoring Rubric: Two Lenses

Every graded answer (drill, mock, grill-prosecute, analyze) gets both lenses.
Log both to coaching-state.md Score History. For calibration anchors — worked
examples of each score level and the reasoning behind it — see `examples.md`.

## Lens 1: Delivery (how she answered)

Score the five dimensions 1-5 using the anchors and root-cause taxonomy in
`rubrics-detailed.md`:

- Substance, Structure, Relevance, Credibility, Differentiation

Then apply all seven checks from `seven-checks.md` as the diagnostic layer:
length (~200 word flag, three-sentence rule), intended bullets landed,
keywords for THIS JD/interviewer, impact position (first or second sentence),
junior signals, numbers under pressure, survivability under three follow-ups.
The checks explain WHY a dimension scored low; the dimensions make sessions
comparable over time.

Output shape per answer (from seven-checks.md): the interviewer's private
verdict in their voice quoting her exact words, only the checks that fired
with quote and fix, then ONE sharper spoken version (2-3 sentences, a shape
to hit, not a script).

## Lens 2: Coverage (did she say what they were listening for)

Score 10 / 7-8 / 5-6 per `local/hm-panel-checklist.md` anchors for whichever
dimension the question tests:

- AI Ecosystem Understanding
- AI Concepts Knowledge
- AI Product Building Experience (UX/taste, evals/responsible AI, business)
- Collaboration and Influence

Check the answer against the expected frame:
- Technical/system questions -> `answer-frames.md` (7-step story structure,
  TRUST, RAG vs fine-tune vs prompting rules, safety pipelines, harness)
- Product sense questions -> `product-sense-frames.md` (P1-P6 moves)

Name the missing indicator explicitly: "You never said golden dataset or
test harness. The HM's checklist has a blank next to evals."

## Reading the two lenses together

- High delivery + low coverage = content gap. Fix: study the frame, re-drill
  the same question next session.
- Low delivery + high coverage = performance gap. Fix: ladder or pushback
  drills on the same story.
- Both low on a dimension across 2+ sessions = append to
  interview-limitations.md and weight future questions there.

## Session-level readout

On "stop" or transcript zoom-out: two strongest answers (quote, why, keep),
two weakest, the ONE habit that cost the most, per-interviewer advance/hold
verdict, and exactly one thing to fix before the next round.
